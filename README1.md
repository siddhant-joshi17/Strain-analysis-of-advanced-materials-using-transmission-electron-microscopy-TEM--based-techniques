# Strain-analysis-of-advanced-materials-using-transmission-electron-microscopy-TEM--based-techniques
TEM-based techniques such as geometric phase analysis (GPA), nano-beam electron diffraction (NBED) can map elastic strain in the advanced nanostructured materials at the nanometer scale. GPA utilizes a high-resolution scanning/TEM image. A phase map of the image is obtained by applying a suitable mask to the image's Fourier transform and then taking its inverse Fourier transform. The phase map is analyzed to generate a nanoscale strain map. On the other hand, NBED is a diffraction-based strain mapping technique. Sub-pixel accuracy is required in locating the centroid of diffraction discs. This is achieved by application of image processing techniques such as edge detection followed by circular Hough transformation, template matching, etc. The goal of this project is to generate reliable, highly accurate nanoscale strain maps of nanostructured materials, such as semiconductor heterostructures for optoelectronic applications. The ability to generate and interpret such nanoscale strain map is critical for strain-engineered materials and devices used in microelectronic industry.
